# Testing plan: `plan-id` / `version`

This is a starting format. Replace prompts with selected evidence, proposals or
explicit unknowns; combine small sections where useful. Follow the shared contract
in the planning guide. The saved plan belongs to the consumer, not this package asset.

## Scope and strategy

| Requirement / cohort | In scope, excluded or unresolved; rationale/decision | Existing designed coverage | Proposed work / gap | Executed evidence and limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Selected requirement and required population | Preserve accepted exclusions and proposed reductions separately | Current case/step/expectation mapping or unknown | Planned coverage, not new cases | Compatible run reference or unknown |

| Risk ID / source parents | Likelihood / impact rationale | Priority and basis | Coverage/work reference | Levels / layers / types / techniques; regression depth / cadence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Count units/populations, cohort denominators and exclusions: state explicitly when
counts are useful. Keep planned, designed and executed views distinct; deduplicate
references and leave missing denominators unknown.

## Activities, capacity and prerequisites

| Activity / sequence | Owner or ownership gap | Dependencies / data / access / readiness / evidence retention | Effort range and basis | Elapsed window / uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Useful cycle activity | Provided owner, proposed role or unknown | Current evidence vs unresolved prerequisite | Person-effort; included/excluded contingency | Timebox and sequencing, not a booking |

Capacity basis, effort total/range, contingency and feasibility: identify supplied
availability and bottlenecks. When a basis is missing, record the estimate decision
and next owner instead of inventing precision. Preserve deferred work and residual risk.

## Decisions, revision and handoff

| Decision / affected IDs | Accepted fact, proposal or unknown | Actual owner / evidence / version, or next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope, priority, target, estimate or other material choice | Preserve accepted vs proposed status | Do not invent acceptance; identify remaining decision |

Changes from prior baseline: affected source/scope/risk/criterion IDs, old/new
meaning, reduced scope/estimates and the decisions requiring review. Preserve IDs
across reordering and link superseded items; retain the prior file.

Handoff: exact plan/version and acceptance basis; selected scope/exclusions;
source/build/environment/cohorts; risk/criterion IDs; evidence references; remaining
owners/actions and residual risk. This plan does not execute the next workflow,
change tracker status, create cases or establish automation readiness.
